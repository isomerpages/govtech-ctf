<h2>Participation categories and eligibility</h2>
<p>STACK the Flags is open to all and the competition is divided into three
categories:</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Open;</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Universities and Polytechnics; and</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Junior Colleges, Integrated Programmes and Institutes of Technical Education.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p>All participating teams can have a maximum of 4 team members. <strong>To be eligible for prizes</strong>,
teams requires at least 1 Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident in the
team.</p>
<p>Employees of GovTech and officers seconded to GovTech are not eligible
to participate.</p>
<p>Participants under the age of 18 at the start date of the Competition
must obtain proper parental/guardian’s consent to participate in the Competition.
The parental/guardian consent form can be found <a href="https://github.com/isomerpages/govtech-jagathestack/blob/staging/files/STACK%20the%20Flags%20Consent%20and%20Indemnity%20Form.docx" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>here</u></a> and
must be submitted before the competition starts.</p>
<p>Teams that fail to comply with the above-mentioned requirements can be
automatically disqualified from the Competition.</p>
