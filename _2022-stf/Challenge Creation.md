chance to do so and win cash prizes!</p>

<p><em>If your challenge is selected, you will be awarded </em><strong><em>SGD 1,000</em></strong><em> per challenge.</em>
</p>
<p>To submit a challenge, please follow the <a href="https://help.hackthebox.com/en/articles/5676859-challenge-submission-requirements" rel="nofollow" target="_blank"><u>challenge submission requirements</u></a>.
For reference, you may refer to this <a href="https://github.com/hackthebox/public-templates/blob/master/examples/RE.md" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>example</u></a>.
Submissions that do not conform to the challenge submission requirements
will not be accepted.</p>

<p>To submit a challenge, click <a href="https://form.gov.sg/6315af7e0ba1c90013c6a643" rel="nofollow" target="_blank"><u>here</u></a>.

documentation, and the <a href="https://github.com/isomerpages/govtech-jagathestack/blob/staging/_2022_STF/%5Cfiles%5CTnC_Community_Sourced_Challenges.pdf" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>signed T&amp;C</u></a>.</p>
