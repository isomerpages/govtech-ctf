<p>Q: What if I have more than 4 friends who want to join the competition?</p>
<p>
<br>A: We strongly encourage you and your friends to divide yourselves into
teams of up to 4 members. Please ensure that there is at least one Singapore
Citizen/Permanent Resident in the team with a local residential address
to be eligible for prizes. The Organising Committee will require each team
to comply strictly to this requirement. This is to ensure fair competition
play and ease of administration for the CTF. Do note that the top 3 winners
from each category will go through a video interview to validate the CTF
experience.</p>
